About

My research interest lies in geometric analysis, complex Geometry, and algebraic geometry (specially focus on canonical Kähler metrics, pluripotential theory, and their relations to algebraic geometry). Recently, I am pondering about Kähler quantization in degenerate setting.

Outside mathematics I follow football, especially FC Barcelona and Manchester City, and keep records of films, anime, live performances, and games. This site keeps the academic material close at hand while leaving a small trace of those side interests.
