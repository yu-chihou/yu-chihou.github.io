University of Maryland

Student Complex Geometry Seminar

Fall 2026

This semester, the Student Complex Geometry Seminar (SGCS) will focus on Morse Theory.

The plan is to cover the basics of Morse theory in the first half of the semester and then move on to more advanced topics.

Reference: J. W. Milnor, Morse Theory, Annals of Mathematics Studies, No. 51, Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ, 1963 (unofficial printing).
