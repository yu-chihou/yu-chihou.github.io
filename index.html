Research

Publications and notes

Preprint, arXiv:2512.11523, submitted

Quantization for Semipositive Adjoint Line Bundles

Studies Donaldson's quantized Monge-Ampere energy in the big and semipositive adjoint setting, using non-pluripolar Monge-Ampere theory and adjoint Bergman kernels.

Bull. Inst. Math. Acad. Sin. (N.S.) 17 (2022), no. 1, 1-51

Asymptotic of Bergman Kernel

A proof of pointwise asymptotic expansion for Bergman kernels on the positive part of certain semi-positive line bundles, based on semi-classical symbolic calculus.

Research note

Localization on P2

Notes from a localization calculation in Gromov-Witten theory, developed as part of a MoST research assistant report.
