Book Review

Elected American: From Red China to Blue Maryland

Lily Qi · 2026 · Read August 2026

An engaging account of how a first-generation immigrant from China, who lived through the Cultural Revolution, became a figure in Maryland Democratic politics.

Qi’s perspective and experiences challenged many of my assumptions about first-generation Asian immigrants in the United States. Her story is also a good reminder of what politics actually is, giving me a glimmer of hope for democracy in this polarized era.

At a time when everyone is talking about 東升西降, her words may sound clichéd, but they still moved me:

“You can be economically successful in China or in any other country. But only in America can you run for office and get elected by people who can’t even pronounce your last name.”
